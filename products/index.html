The Pipeline

Four agent roles, in sequence

Each mission runs as a chain of specialized agents on the OpenClaw framework (Claude Opus reasoning over open sources such as PubMed, Europe PMC, ClinicalTrials.gov, Open Targets and FDA FAERS, reached through the Harvard ToolUniverse toolkit and purpose-built connectors). The run is fail-closed at the report level: a report does not ship if fewer than 75% of its cited identifiers trace to retrieved evidence, if it cites none, if the run retrieved no evidence, if a cited paper is found retracted, or if a registry says a cited identifier does not exist.

01

Systematic Database Scan

Scout

Systematic scan of peer-reviewed literature, trial registries, and FDA drug-label and adverse-event data, with identifiers normalized across systems (PMID, PMCID, NCT, ISRCTN, DOI, PubChem CID, UniProt).

02

Quantitative Signal Analysis

Analyst

Disproportionality metrics (ROR, PRR), drug-interaction matrices, and pharmacokinetic / pharmacodynamic profiling with molecular-level specificity.

03

Evidence Synthesis

Synthesizer

Integrates scout and analyst outputs into a signal hierarchy: contradiction resolution, confidence-tier assignment, and strategic recommendations for research and diligence use.

04

Citation Verification

Quality Audit

Checks every cited identifier against the evidence actually retrieved at runtime, by type and number; screens cited papers for retraction against PubMed and Crossref; re-checks each identifier at its issuing registry and records the result in a reproducibility manifest.

How verification actually works

Citations aren't taken on faith. Each cited identifier is matched, by type and number, to the records the engine actually retrieved during the run: a report with fewer than 75% of its cited identifiers traced does not ship, and any untraced identifier is flagged [UNVERIFIED] at every mention. Cited papers are screened for retraction against PubMed and Crossref, and any paper the screen could not reach is named. Each identifier is then re-checked at its issuing public registry; one the registry says does not exist blocks delivery. Every report carries a citation-integrity ledger with a grounding grade, a reproducibility manifest, and a SHA-256 checksum for every file. These checks confirm that each source exists and was retrieved, not that it supports the sentence citing it.
