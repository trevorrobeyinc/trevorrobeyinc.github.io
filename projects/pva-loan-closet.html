PVA Mobility Equipment Loan Closet

A medical-equipment lending system for a volunteer ambulance service — built on Google AppSheet, Google Sheets, and Google Apps Script, with a mocked Little Green Light CRM integration. Everything below is the actual project: the architecture, the documents, and the full build guide, shown here rather than linked away.

Project source Read the build guide

01Overview

Staff track inventory, loans, returns, donations, and support tickets in one app; automated reminders chase overdue equipment, and each donation syncs to the donor CRM. The design is deliberately three-layer: AppSheet is the interface and event automation, Google Sheets is the system of record, and Apps Script owns integration, secrets, and scheduled jobs. It's a cross-functional piece — software and operations rather than marketing — included here to show range.

  • Match-before-create CRM sync
  • Mock / live integration flag
  • 11/11 automated tests
  • Scheduled reminder cadence
  • Relative-dated demo data
  • Fail-closed webhook

Status: the backend, the Apps Script integration, the test suite, and the build guide are complete; the AppSheet front end assembles from the guide below.
